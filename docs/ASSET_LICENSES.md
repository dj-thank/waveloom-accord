# Asset provenance and release ledger

Audit date: 2026-07-21 (Asia/Tokyo)

This ledger is the release gate for bundled visual and audio assets. Runtime assets
must have a direct source page, a verified license, a content hash, and a clear
gameplay role. Imported meshes are decorative by default; collision is authored
separately from the competitive map blueprint.

## Project-authored audio

The 18 weapon sounds and 72 ability sounds under `assets-src/local-audio/` are
generated by `tools/generate_local_audio_assets.js`. The generator uses only
project-authored deterministic DSP code and the canonical hero/action IDs. It
does not download, sample, embed, or infer through third-party audio, model
weights, or services. The authoritative audio manifest records the generator
version, fixed seed, synthesis profile, WAV format, byte count, and SHA-256 for
every source/runtime pair. The generated audio therefore follows the
project's source-code distribution terms; no separate third-party audio notice
is required.

The two earlier ElevenLabs MP3 experiments remain as non-authoritative source
history. They are not referenced by the integrated hero asset SSOT or runtime
and are not evidence for the admitted 90-file local DSP catalog.

## Admission policy

- Prefer CC0. CC BY is accepted only with complete author/source/license notice.
- Reject NC, ND, Editorial, unknown, or model-page-only license claims for a
  redistributable production bundle. CC BY-SA/GPL assets require a separate
  compatibility review and are not accepted by default.
- Record original and converted SHA-256 values, conversion tool/version,
  texture resizing, LOD generation, and all attribution obligations.
- A downloaded mesh cannot create authoritative collision implicitly. Every
  gameplay surface must be compiled from the shared map blueprint and tested
  against the visible presentation surface.
